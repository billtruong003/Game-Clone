Arrow Out Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026 · Developer: Bill The Dev

In short
  • Arrow Out has no accounts or login, and we (the developer) do not collect your personal data.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads through AppLovin MAX (with Google AdMob as a mediated network). These ad companies collect some device data, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads.
  • If you live in a region where consent is required (for example the EEA, UK or Switzerland), you are asked before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options.

1. Who we are

Arrow Out (the "App") is a casual mobile game published on Google Play by Bill The Dev, an individual developer ("we", "us"). It is a calm puzzle game where you tap arrows with a clear path to the edge of the board so they fly out.

This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, who processes it, and the choices you have. It applies only to the App, not to other apps or websites.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and the App has no account system.

The App stores a small amount of game data only on your device (using the device's local app storage), for example: levels cleared, stars earned, hint count, endless and daily-challenge best scores, and whether the tutorial was seen, sound/music/vibration settings, whether you own "Remove ads", and when the review prompt was last shown. This data is never sent to us. We do not run our own analytics or tracking.

3. Information collected by third parties

The App includes software development kits (SDKs) from the companies below. They act independently or as our service providers and handle data according to their own privacy policies.

3.1 Advertising: AppLovin MAX and Google AdMob

The App shows full-screen ads between rounds and optional rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch, for example a hint or an extra heart). Ads are served through AppLovin MAX, a mediation platform that can also request ads from Google AdMob. These SDKs may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and app set ID;
  • Device information such as model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size and mobile carrier;
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate (country or city level) location;
  • App interaction data related to ads, such as ad views, clicks, video completions and app launches;
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information of the ad SDK.

They use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted and with consent where required, show personalized ads.

Learn more:

  • AppLovin Privacy Policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3.2 In-app purchase: Google Play Billing

You can buy "Remove ads", a one-time purchase that turns off the between-round ads (optional rewarded ads stay available if you want the reward). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid so the App can unlock the feature.

3.3 Ratings: Google Play In-App Review

From time to time the App may show Google Play's built-in rating dialog. This is provided by Google; we do not receive any information about whether you rated the App or what you wrote.

5. Consent and how to change it

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, provided through AppLovin's consent flow) before ads are requested. You can accept or decline personalized advertising. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads, which are based on context such as the App itself and your general location.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the App, go to Settings → Privacy options. (This button appears for players in regions where consent applies.) A link to this policy is also available at Settings → Privacy policy.

6. Your choices about ads

  • Opt out of personalized ads: on most Android devices go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID. Menu names can differ between devices.
  • Remove between-round ads: buy "Remove ads" in the App.
  • Stop all processing: uninstall the App.

7. Data retention

Game data stored on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We do not keep a copy. Data collected by AppLovin and Google is kept according to their own retention policies (see the links above). AppLovin states that device-associated information is often kept for less than two years or until deletion is requested.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and its target audience is ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the App in a way that caused personal information to be collected by our ad partners, please contact us and we will help you ask the relevant partner to delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information:

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): the right to know, access and delete personal information and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Ad partners' use of your advertising ID and IP address may be considered "sharing" under these laws; you can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID as described in section 6 and by using the partners' tools.

Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, most requests are best directed to the ad partners that hold it:

  • AppLovin privacy requests: see https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can also contact us and we will do our best to help.

10. Security

Game data is kept in the App's private storage on your device. The ad and billing SDKs we use send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose well-established providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

11. International transfers

Our ad partners may process data in the United States and other countries. They are responsible for applying appropriate safeguards for such transfers as described in their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the App adds new features or partners. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be reflected in the App's Google Play listing.
